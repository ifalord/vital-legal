Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026
Operator: IFA- TECH LLC, 7950 NW 53rd St, Suite 342, Doral, FL 33166, USA
Contact: vital-app@ifa-tech.com

This policy applies to the consumer health data of consumers covered by the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and by Nevada law (NRS 603A.400 to 603A.550). It says what consumer health data Vital collects and why, where it comes from, who receives it, and how you can exercise your rights. Because Vital is a health app, almost everything it keeps about you is consumer health data. Our Privacy Policy describes all of Vital's data practices.

Vital does not collect your precise location or genetic data. It does not keep your voice recordings or photos on its servers and does not use them to identify you.

2. Where it comes from

  • You, when you enter it in the app or talk to the assistant.
  • Your caregivers, if you allow it: the medications they propose for your list (they go on it only if you add them) and their comments on alerts.
  • Your phone: time zone, language, notification token, IP address and device type.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple: an identifier for your account, your email or private relay address and, the first time only, your name.
  • Vital's own systems: dose statuses recorded automatically (a dose not marked 2 hours after its time), adherence and streak.
  • AI services, only if you turn on AI features: transcriptions, medication information, interaction ratings, pill and prescription readings, patterns, suggestions and summaries.

4. Your rights

  • Confirm and access. Ask whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data and get a copy of it, with the list of all third parties and affiliates with whom we shared or sold it and an email address or online way to contact each one (Section 3 names them; we send you their contact details with our answer). In the app, Settings → Privacy and data → “Download my data” gives you a file with your data; what the file cannot include, we send you by email when you ask.
  • Withdraw consent and stop collection or sharing. Turn off AI features in Settings → Privacy and data: from then on nothing more is sent to AI services (a change made without a connection reaches our server when the phone is back online). Change or remove a caregiver's access in Caregiver → Your caregivers. To stop all collection, delete your account.
  • Delete. In the app, Settings → Privacy and data → “Delete my account”, or ask us by email. Deleting your account erases your consumer health data from our database at once; the copies in our database provider's backups are deleted as those backups expire, within 7 days. We keep only a record of what you accepted (each document, its version and the date, with your account's identifier and your email as a keyed hash, and no health data) for 5 years, apart from everything else, to answer a legal claim.
  • Review and correct. You can edit your entries and your profile in the app at any time, or ask us by email to correct them.

How to make a request

Use the options in the app above, or write to vital-app@ifa-tech.com from the email address of your Vital account, with the subject “Consumer health data request”, saying what you want. You do not need to create an account. If we cannot confirm that the request comes from you, we may ask you for information that lets us verify it, and we use it only for that. Requests are free.

We answer without undue delay and within 45 days of receiving your request. If we need more time, we may extend that once by 45 more days, and we will tell you why within the first 45 days. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days of confirming the request comes from you; deleting your account in the app removes it from our database at once.

How to appeal our decision

If we refuse your request, in whole or in part, you can appeal by replying to our answer, or by writing to vital-app@ifa-tech.com with the subject “Appeal”. Within 45 days of receiving your appeal we will tell you in writing what we did or did not do, and why. If we deny your appeal, you can file a complaint with the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov/file-complaint) or the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov).

5. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. We will not collect, use or share consumer health data of new categories, for new purposes or with new categories of recipients without first updating this policy and asking for your consent.
