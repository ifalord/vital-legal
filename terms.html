3. Reminders and alerts

Reminders and alerts help you, but they are not guaranteed. Reminders are scheduled on your phone: they reach you only if Vital may send notifications and the phone is on, and they sound only if its settings (Silent mode, Focus, Do Not Disturb) let them. After a change made on another phone, they are set up once Vital is opened on your phone. Signing out removes the reminders from that phone.

Alerts to your caregivers are sent by our servers when a dose is marked as skipped, and about 2 hours after the time of a dose that has not been marked (the latter only once your phone has opened Vital and told our server its time zone). They reach your caregivers only if our servers, Apple's and Expo's notification services and their phone work. After 2 days with no dose recorded, caregivers get one notice instead of one per dose. Vital only knows what is marked in the app; it cannot tell whether a medicine was actually taken.

Vital is not an emergency service and does not monitor anyone. Do not rely on Vital alone for doses where a delay could cause serious harm (for example insulin, anticoagulants or antiepileptics): keep another reminder and agree with your caregivers how you will reach each other. In an emergency, call 911 in the United States or your local emergency number.
