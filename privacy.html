1.2 Data we collect automatically

  • Device identifier: push notification token (Apple/Google) — not the device's hardware ID.

1.3 Data we do NOT collect

  • GPS location.
  • Contact list.
  • Browsing history.
  • Financial information (the application does not currently collect any payment).

Vital processes health data, which most privacy laws classify as sensitive personal information requiring heightened protection. Your legal basis for this processing is:

  • You can also withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account, which erases all of your data.

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption in transit: all communications use TLS 1.2+.
  • Encryption at rest: Supabase encrypts the database with AES-256.
  • Strong hashing: passwords are stored using bcrypt.
  • Per-user isolation (RLS): Row Level Security policies ensure each user can only access their own data.
  • Restricted access: no employee has routine access to individual health data.
  • Auditing: we log access and sensitive changes.
  • Backups: daily, encrypted, with 7-day retention.
  • Breach notification: in the event of a security breach that creates a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant authority within 72 hours and notify you without undue delay, in accordance with applicable laws (including the GDPR Articles 33–34 for EU residents and U.S. state breach notification statutes for U.S. residents).

11. Artificial intelligence

Vital uses AI models provided by OpenRouter, which routes to models such as Google Gemini, Moonshot Kimi K2, and others. The data you send to the assistant (text, audio, photos) is processed in real time to generate the response and is not used to train models, under our current agreement with OpenRouter and the policies of the selected models.

11.1 Your permission

AI features send part of your information to AI services run by other companies. Until you allow them, nothing is sent and the AI features stay off.

  • It is your explicit consent, separate from this Policy. You give it inside the app, in its own prompt, before the first AI feature runs. One permission covers all AI features. Accepting this Policy does not give it.
  • It is recorded. We save each decision (allowed or turned off) with its date and the version of the explanation shown to you.
  • You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy and data, without deleting your account. From then on nothing more is sent. Withdrawing cannot recall information that has already been processed.

11.2 What each AI feature sends

Once you have allowed AI features, these are the features that send information to OpenRouter, and what each one sends. Some run by themselves; the rest run when you use them.

  • Assistant chat. What you type to the assistant and the recent messages of the conversation (up to the last 30), including the confirmations of anything the assistant records for you. It runs when you send a message.
  • Voice dictation. The recording of your voice, to turn it into text. It runs when you dictate with the microphone.
  • Pill and prescription photos. The photo you take or choose. A prescription photo can show your name and your doctor's. It runs when you take or choose a photo to identify a pill or read a prescription.
  • Medication information and interaction checks. The name of the medication you look up, or the names of your medications for an interaction check. Doses and other details are not sent. They run when you open a medication's information or the interaction check, and an interaction check also runs by itself when you save a new medication, to compare it with your others.
  • Daily suggestions. A snapshot of your day: how many of today's doses were taken, skipped, missed or are still pending; today's mood; whether you logged pain today; your adherence over the last 7 days; and the symptoms and pain you logged in the last 3 days. It runs by itself when you open Vital.
  • Pattern detection. Your saved medications (name, dose and start date) and, for the last 30 days, the status of each scheduled dose, your symptoms, pain entries, mood, stress and energy check-ins, and manual measurements such as blood pressure or glucose. It runs by itself in the background after you save a medication or a measurement, or when the assistant records a medication, symptom, pain entry or measurement for you, and whenever you ask for it from the Health tab.
  • Report summary. From the medical report for the last 30 days: adherence figures, your medications (name, dose and times of day), symptoms and pain entries with their dates, and your average mood. Before the data is sent on, our server keeps only these fields, so your name, email and account ID are left out. It runs by itself when you open the medical report.

AI responses may contain errors and never constitute medical advice. Read the full Medical Disclaimer.
